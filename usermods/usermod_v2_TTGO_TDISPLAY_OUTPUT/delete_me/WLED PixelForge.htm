Available Tokens

#TIME - HH:MM AM/PM

#DATE - DD.MM.YYYY

#MMDD - Month/Day
#YYYY - Year

#MO - Month number

#DAY - Weekday (Mon)
#DDDD - Weekday (Monday)
Tips:
• Mix text and tokens: "It's #HHMM O'Clock" or "#HH:#MM:#SS"
• Add '0' suffix for leading zeros: #TIME0, #HH0, etc.
